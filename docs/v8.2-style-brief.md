# v8.2 style brief: bring every screen into the command-center language

The owner loved the new Home (`v6/s12_home.js`, `v6/m_home.css`). Your job is to give your screens the same look and the same clarity. Presentation only: no change to data, finance math or behaviour. Read `docs/v8-builder-brief.md` (rules, verification, commit) and `CLAUDE.md` first, then open the demo and look at Home for the owner, the accountant and an agent (desktop and 390 px).

## The language
1. **Page header band** on every top-level page: `hcPage({eyebrow, title, brief, ctas, kpis, cls, aria, extra})` from `s12_home.js`.
   - `eyebrow`: the sidebar section name (Sales, Catalog, Money, Help, Admin), uppercase mint.
   - `title`: the page name. `brief`: ONE sentence that says what matters on this page right now, computed from data, with the key figures emphasized (below).
   - `ctas`: at most two, built with `hcCta('mint'|'ghost', iconName, label, 'data-act="..." data-v="..."')`. The mint one is the page's single primary action.
   - `kpis`: 2 to 4 tiles `{lab, val (already formatted), sub, cls:'pf'|'warn', act, v, pt}`; a tile with `act` is a button that drills to the screen or filter that explains it.
   - Replace the old page title (`head()`) with this band. Keep every existing control that was in the old header (search, filters, "New" buttons) either in `ctas` or in the first card below.
2. **Body**: `<div class="hc-grid">` with `<div class="hc-c7">`, `<div class="hc-c5">`, `<div class="hc-c12">` columns (single column on phones automatically). Cards: `<div class="card hc-card">` with a header `<div class="hc-h"><h3>Title</h3><span class="hc-pill red|green|blue">…</span> or <span class="muted small">…</span></div>`. Links to more: `<button class="hc-more" …>Label ${ic('chev','flip')}</button>`.
3. **Lists of actions**: the `.hc-steps` pattern (dot by severity, bold text, small context line, one button). **Photo rails**: `.hc-rail` with `.hc-dep` cards. **Rings**: `hcRing(pct,size,cls)`.
4. **Tables** keep `.tbl` / `.al-tbl` inside `.scroll`; give them a card. Long tables: show the first 8 rows and an "and N more" link.
5. **Color emphasis in text** (owner decision): `hlNeg(x)` red for a negative outcome (money lost, late, overdue, urgent), `hlPos(x)` green for a positive one (recovered, collected, on target), `hlWarn(x)` amber for money still open, `hlKey(x)` bold neutral for the main figure. Only the key figure of a sentence, at most two or three per sentence. Inside the dark header band they switch to light variants automatically.
6. **Empty states**: say why it is empty and offer the next action; hide a card entirely when it would only say "nothing here" and nothing can be done from it.
7. **Mobile first**: check every screen at 390 px. No horizontal page scroll; wide tables scroll inside their card.
8. **Night mode and Hebrew**: tokens only (`var(--…)`), logical CSS, real Hebrew through `L(he,en)`.
